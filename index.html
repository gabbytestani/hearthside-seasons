About

Hearthside Seasons is a website created out of fascination for cozy, otherworldly realms. Originally inspired by the Yule log asmr scene craze, this website is a portal for new and exciting holidays, seasons, and celebrations.

Sit back and explore these different villages and indoor settings as one would a LoFi video, and make sure to follow its creator, Gabriella Testani, on her socials for more Claude coded content.
